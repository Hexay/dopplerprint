# Power-domain LLR scoring (stage 2), 2026-11-03

Stage 2 of `matching_mathematical_methods.md`: `[search.score] mode = "power_llr"` (default stays `"zsum"`).
Code: `search/power_llr/{mod,plane,sum}.rs`. Base 0ca15f1. All runs are CPU, RAYON_NUM_THREADS = 4, on a box
at load 30+, so wall times are noisy; the CPU-s ratios are the comparison.

## What it computes

| Piece | Choice |
|---|---|
| Plane | linear power from the 1 s Hann window, x = P/N̂, with N̂ = running q25 · κ_b (`whiten`'s floor in linear form). κ_b is the null mean/q25, measured per bin on 60 s of seeded white noise on the same grid. Max-pooled log bins are not Exp(1): above ~35 Hz a log bin pools 2–29 zero-padded FFT bins. Frames are re-centred like `whiten`. x is capped at 30, not clipped at input. |
| Per-pixel LLR | profiled (default): ŝ = m − 1 is fitted per (5 s segment, harmonic, f0 step), with m = mean x. The term is n(m − 1 − ln m) when m > 1 and 0 otherwise, which is the Exp LLR at its own ŝ (weight ŝ/(1+ŝ)). A null mean of 0.25 is subtracted per term. A faded harmonic or segment costs 0, not a veto. |
| Fixed-prior variant | `llr.segment_s = 0`: Σ w_h·x − ln(1+ŝ_h), w_h = ŝ_h/(1+ŝ_h), with ŝ_h fixed (`llr.snr`). Kept as an ablation (negative result below). |
| n_eff | each sum ÷ 2.2. Measured on noise-only audio, arm (a) of the overlap question: Var(Σ_40 frames)/(40·Var x) = **2.22**, against ≈1.9 predicted. Test `noise_only_plane_is_mean_one_and_overlap_factor_near_two`: mean x 0.997. |
| f0 | log-mean-exp over the f0 slide (uniform prior), not max. The reported f0 is the argmax. |
| Clock | whole-frame sweep as today, then 2 bisection levels (±½, ±¼ frame) around the best. Offsets are interpolated linearly in time between frames, giving 0.0625 s resolution. Test: true clock between frames, recovered to −0.037 s. |
| Units | `EventScore.z` / `JointScore.total` are **nats** in this mode. |

The units change matters downstream, and I did not recalibrate anything here because likelihood/modes/T belong to another agent:

- `assoc.silence_penalty` is 3 "per unexplained flight". That is 3 z under zsum but only 3 nats here, where truth scores run into the hundreds of nats, so the penalty is effectively off.
- `eval.posterior_beta` and the fitted T assume z.
- `app::report::MIN_EVENT_Z` = 4 compares against `e.z`.

## Integration (CUDA-safe)

`power_llr` is a separate CPU scorer over the same `job::Job`s. It is not a re-score after `job::assemble`, because it changes what is summed per pixel. It never touches `sweep.rs`, `fixed.rs` or `gpu/`.

- `Scorer::new`: with mode power_llr, `Auto` and `Cpu` give a CPU scorer and `Cuda` returns an error.
- `Scorer::score` and `score::score_joint` each make one mode branch per call.
- With mode zsum the path is byte-identical. Pre-rebase, all 60 dev-corpus + sim case JSONs matched main minus `runtime_s` (run `id_base` vs `id_new`).

## Pre-registered primary: SNR ladder (experiments/sim_snr, `tools/gen_sim_snr.py`)

The ladder has 10 single-aircraft geometries × 12 source levels: 0 to −22 dB in 2 dB steps below a reference where the fundamental is +16 dB in 1 Hz at CPA. Each case also has two silent ADS-B-only distractors with similar speed and height, passing 1.5–4 km away with CPA within ±1.5 s. The clock prior is ±2 s. Metric: the level at which truth_tuple_rank = 1 in ≥ 80% of cases, i.e. the lowest level before the first rung that falls below 80%.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| zsum (`snr_zsum`) | .9 .7 .9 .7 .7 .7 .8 .5 .5 .5 .6 .6 | **0 dB** (−1 interpolated; non-monotone) | 63 1036 440 335 851 532 | 1052 |
| power_llr (`snr_llr`) | 1 1 1 .9 .9 .8 .8 .5 .5 .5 .6 .5 | **−12 dB** | 327 104 92 457 504 304 | 2755 |

The pre-registered target (≥ 2 dB lower) is **met**: about 10 dB lower with the penalty-scale confound removed.

Paired rank-1 flips, power_llr vs zsum:

| comparison | 0 … −10 dB: power_llr gains / loses | −12 … −22 dB: gains / loses |
|---|---|---|
| default penalty | **10 / 0** (46 → 56 of 60) | 7 / 8 |
| penalty 0 | **14 / 2** (44 → 56) | 11 / 17 |

Below −12 dB both methods sit at ~0.5, which is chance among the truth and two distractors. Neither recovers there.

`compare snr_zsum snr_llr`, 120 pairs:

- error Δ mean −256 m [−492, −23], median −4 m; 65 better / 48 worse
- flights correct +17 / −8
- clock error unchanged

Log score, score gap and 90% coverage are not comparable across modes (z vs nats at β = 1).

Some of zsum's high-SNR rank-1 misses come from the silence penalty (g06: truth raw 50.6 > distractor 49.5, but truth carries −3 for the unexplained distractor). Others are near-ties (g02: 45.99 vs 45.93). The penalty-0 rows remove the penalty confound and power_llr still wins.

## Per-pixel SNR on the real corpus (`eval --pixel-snr`)

Method: at the truth observer and truth flight, the clock and f0 come from the zsum sweep. Each harmonic's curve is then read on x. ŝ = mean x − 1 per 5 s segment. The table gives quantiles over all segments, in dB.

| | 4 | ≤0 | −5.9 | 0.3 | 6.4 | 0.26 |
| | 5 | ≤0 | −6.1 | −3.0 | 0.2 | 0.13 |
| | 6 | ≤0 | ≤0 | ≤0 | ≤0 | 0.00 |
| Oyster Bay (2 events) | 1 | 5.3 | 11.7 | 16.6 | 21.5 | 0.85 |
| | 2–3 | | −0.6 / −7.3 | 6.0 / −0.4 | 12.2 / 7.6 | 0.47 / 0.24 |

Median per-frame z (z-sum weighting along the truth curve) is 0.75 [q25 0.42, q75 1.66] on Livermore and about 2.0 on Oyster Bay. **All 30 events are < 3.**

So the proposed switch rule ("power domain wherever median per-frame z < 3") does not discriminate: it would select every event. Whitening suppresses the near-stationary approach and recede legs, so most frames carry little z even when h1 is +9 dB per pixel.

Harmonics 3–6 are mostly below 0 dB per pixel, consistent with the `tnr` hint. This supports profiling ŝ per harmonic, so that absent harmonics cost nothing, rather than a fixed prior.

## Negative results

- **Fixed-prior ŝ (the brief's w_h ∝ ŝ/(1+ŝ) from a table).** On the synthetic tone test it scored truth −180 nats and pulled the clock 4.6 s when the 60 s window held only a ~20 s audible stretch. Every covered pixel without signal costs w − ln(1+ŝ) < 0, so hypotheses with less coverage win. The profiled form fixes this, and the fixed form is kept only as `llr.segment_s = 0`.
- **First SNR ladder (±120 s prior, distractors ±40 s).** Vacuous: rank 1 was 0.9–1.0 at every level for both methods, with errors of km. A single event is degenerate along track and clock under a wide prior, and the distractors never associated. Regenerated as above.
- **Speed.** power_llr costs 2.6–3.7× the zsum CPU, so the target "at least as fast" is **not met**. The causes:
  - f64 lerp per pixel, against fixed-point
  - count bookkeeping
  - 4 extra refinement evaluations, about +24% at the ±2 s sweep

  A difference-array count kept results byte-identical but gave no measurable gain on the loaded box.

## TODO / next steps

- Speed: upsample x ×4 along log-f once per recording, so one plane bin is one f0 step. Snap each curve point to the nearest plane bin (≤ ⅛ native bin error). A frame's whole f0 slide then becomes one contiguous f32 slice add, which vectorises and needs no lerp or conversion. This was prototyped, then dropped for scope.
- Units downstream (other owners): the silence penalty in nats; T/β for nats; `MIN_EVENT_Z`.
- Clock: marginalise δ (Laplace or quadrature at ≤ σ_δ/2 around the refined peak) instead of max. This is stage 5, as the verification notes advise.
- Drift: a per-event f0 drift term in the slide. It is needed because 1 %/min drift gives 150–900 m cross-track bias (`pipeline_bias_measurements.md`). Use the frequency-linear form f0(1 + βτ), not log-linear (`derived_checks.md`). This is stage 5.
- Harmonic set: real events carry a ½ subharmonic and harmonics up to 7 (`corpus_signatures.md`). `ScoreParams.harmonics` is `u32`, so allow fractional indices; this touches `job::harmonic_shifts` and the CUDA shifts.
- Close passes: losing the CPA stretch costs 91% at 0.5 km (coordinator note). The 5 s profile segments let a faded CPA segment cost 0, but they do not up-weight CPA.
- Decision: keep `zsum` as the default. power_llr wins clearly in the −2 … −10 dB band on sim and is neutral on the loud real corpus. Revisit once T is fitted per mode and a faint real set exists.
